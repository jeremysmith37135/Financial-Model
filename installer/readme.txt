================================================================================
OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The CFO Financial Model Generator creates professional financial models from
your QuickBooks Online P&L and Balance Sheet exports. It generates a complete
Excel-based financial model with:

  - P&L Statement with YTD comparisons
  - Balance Sheet
  - Cash Flow Statement
  - Forecast & Budget comparison
  - Executive Dashboard with KPIs
  - Variance analysis with notes

================================================================================
REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

  - Windows 10 or later
  - Microsoft Excel (2016 or later recommended)
  - P&L and Balance Sheet exports from QuickBooks Online (.xlsx format)

================================================================================
QUICK START
================================================================================

1. Export your P&L and Balance Sheet from QuickBooks Online
   - Reports > Profit and Loss > Export to Excel
   - Reports > Balance Sheet > Export to Excel

2. Launch CFO Financial Model Generator

3. Select "Create New Model" and upload your files

4. Choose a save location for your financial model

5. Click "Generate Model" and wait for completion
